EasyEdit

Mobile video editor · Web app — open it and start cutting

EasyEdit is a video editor that runs in your phone's browser: import multiple clips, trim, split and reorder them on a timeline, add speed changes, filters, aspect ratios, stickers and text, then export the finished video. Everything is processed locally in the browser (FFmpeg.wasm) — your videos are never uploaded to any server.

Features

  • Multi-clip import: stitch several videos onto one timeline
  • Timeline editing: trim in/out points, split at the playhead, drag to reorder, delete clips
  • Speed: 8 speed levels; reverse playback (applied on export)
  • Picture: 7 filters, 6 aspect ratios (16:9 / 9:16 / 1:1 …), 90° rotation, zoom push/pull, black / white / blurred border fill
  • Audio: volume control and voice enhancement; extract audio from any clip and save it as MP3, or import audio as BGM
  • Stickers & text: overlay them anywhere on the frame
  • Export: renders the final MP4 locally on your device
  • Works on desktop browsers too: mouse selection, playhead/edge dragging, clip reordering and editing shortcuts
